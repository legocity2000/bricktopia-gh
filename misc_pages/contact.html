Contact us

Give Feedback

You can give us feedback about the site on Discord or trough email: contact.bricktopia@gmail.com

We accept (almost) any type of feedback or suggestion, we'll look at them as soon as we have time for it.

Contributing

If you want to contribute to the site and then contact us via our dedicated Discord server or via email: contact.bricktopia@gmail.com

Coding knowledge is not needed if you can help the site without it (for example with graphic designs or content writing for pages like the Lego News section). An example site can be found in /misc_pages/legonews/example.html (can be checked here)

Join the Team

If you are known on the Bricktopia Discord server then Contact Branden on Discord and you can apply for the Community Contributor role. This role has no moderating powers, but Contributors have a close connection with the team.
