Frequently asked questions

This page is under development, thank you for understanding.

Is the website open-source?

LC20: Yes, the site is open source, we are on Github, you can check out our github page here.

Does the developer team accept contributions?

LC20: Yes, we accept contributions, especially content writers for our Lego News page, but feel free to contact us with anything and we'll take a look at it. Check Contact us for more information!

Can I buy, sell and trade Lego on the Bricktopia Discord server?

LC20: Yes, you can, we have a dedicated marketplace section. Buy and sell sets at your own risk. If you have questions then contact IceCreamMan via Discord.
