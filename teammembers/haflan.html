Haflan

Director of Community security

Introduction

Hello there! My name is Haflan, also known as MrHaflan. I joined Bricktopia in 2024 and was hoping to just find some Lego MOCs to get some inspiration first, but I accidentally discovered something even better. It was the community. I have made friends, memories, and have found beauty in this lovely Lego community. The community cares for and supports each other even in the tough times. I'm lucky to be in this server; my hobby with Lego grew even stronger and I have become happier than ever.

Personality

I love to talk about random topics and love to see other people's creativity. I am friendly, but when things go south, I can get mad. I want to play with Lego more, but due to limited time and money, I can't. To be honest, I don't really know what my personality is, but that is the best I can tell.

What I do

I am one of the admins on the team. My role is the Director of Community Security in the Bricktopia server. I'm the one who is responsible for the community's safety. If you have any problems, just feel free to reach out to me on Discord. If you want to talk about something, you can also reach out to me; I'm happy to help as much as I can.

My connection to Lego

Since I gained self-awareness, I have just loved Lego. I don't know where it exactly started, but what I know is that I have loved Lego since a very young age. I remember watching The Lego Movie for the first time, and it inspired me to create my own Lego creations, which began as a basic car, then a truck, a tank, a helicopter, etc. I live in a country where Lego is so expensive, so I could only afford the fake ones, but I'm still happy because it is not the brand that matters, but the toy and your imagination. Growing up, I'm trying my best to get real Lego using my own money.

Etc

I'm also a fan of an indie series called MURDER DRONES. I'm a beginner digital artist and an architecture student. I have a dream to become a game dev and make games with the most cinematic stories. I love writing, singing, taking pictures, enjoying nature, and much more.

Motto

"Life is hard, but whatever you do, never give up, because everything you are going through will be an important ingredient to create the best version of yourself."
