SirIceCreamMan

Marketplace operations officer

Introduction

Hello, I'm SirIceCreamMan, or just "IceCream" for short. I have been completely obsessed with LEGO ever since I was young. I joined Bricktopia to handle the outreach and strategy, bringing my love for the brick to our growing community.

Personality

I like to keep things fun, creative, and highly collaborative. I am passionate about the LEGO hobby, always looking for unique ways to connect builders, and I rarely turn down a good joke—or a scoop of ice cream.

What I do

I am an admin, the marketplace operations director of Bricktopia. Outside of the server, I run SirIceCreamMansBrixNFigs, an active BrickLink store where I manage inventory, source rare parts, and help builders worldwide finish their custom MOCs.

My connection to Lego

My journey started at a very young age, building whatever I could imagine out of basic bricks. Over the years, that childhood passion turned into a massive personal collection and eventually evolved into running my own brick-selling business.

Etc

When I am not sorting parts or managing marketing campaigns, I love tracking down rare minifigures and looking at awesome custom builds. Outside of LEGO, I am a huge aquarium hobbyist with a massive love for keeping aquatic snails and shrimp. I also enjoy standard nerd stuff, gaming, and analyzing the ever-changing LEGO economy.

Motto

"Do what you love, love what you do, and deliver more than you promise." — Harvey Mackay
