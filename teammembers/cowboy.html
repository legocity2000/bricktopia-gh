Introduction

Hello y'all! I'm the Cowboy, I construct things IRL and with Lego, and am your local nature enthusiast! I joined this server, originally to pick up some tips and tricks for technic building techniques for my MOC aspirations.

Personality

I'm a battered, rough, sarcastic soul, I'll tease you from minute one, and expect nothing less in return.. humor is my sword and shield.

What I do

I was offered a moderator position kind of randomly, though I suspect im the diversity hire... How many mod teams can claim to have a sentient tree in their ranks? I specialize in keeping chats chill, and occuring in the right place.. just your general purpose helper for any and all small issues

My connection to Lego

3rd generation Lego lover: I got to play with the last generations collection, before beginning to build my own personal collection. I specialize in MOC building, most all sets I buy are specifically for unique and rare pieces they may have. My favorite themes are Atlantis, Architecture, Castle, and Agents.

Etc

my 4th generation lego.lovers, my young nephews, inspire me to revisit my Lego city collection. I have creative passions that far exceed Lego: sketching, sculpting, junk sculpture, wood working, leather working.. I'm a busy guy with lots to do

Motto

A bald head is merely a blank canvas.. you're a Sharpie away from greatness
