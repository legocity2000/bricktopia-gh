Branden

Founder and Leader

What makes you unique?

I'm a small LEGO creator who enjoys sharing fun and creative content with others. For more than two years, I've been dedicated to building, maintaining, and improving this server, always striving to make it a welcoming place for everyone.

What are your interests?

Without a doubt: gaming, LEGO, and photography. After all, those passions are the reason we're all here today! 😉

What are you most proud of?

I'm incredibly proud to lead Bricktopia and to have played such an important role in shaping and developing the community into what it is today.

What do you appreciate most about the community?

One of the things I value most is our honesty and mutual respect. Everyone works together with the goal of creating something truly special, and that shared commitment is what makes our community unique.

Where did your passion for building communities come from?

I grew up in a German city that has a long-standing partnership with a neighboring Polish city. Growing up with that cross-border connection inspired me to bring people together and create a community where everyone feels welcome.
