Introduction

Hi, I'm legocity2000, LC20 or just "Lego" for short. I joined Bricktopia because I moderated side-by-side with Branden on another server and he invited me here. Currently I am the "Director of Channel Operations" or just "Channel Manager" for short.

My connection with Lego

I started young, with Duplo, then the real passion started when I got my first City set at the age of 4. Since that I'm a big collector of new and "old" (~2010) sets.

What do I do outside of Bricktopia and Lego?

I struggle with life, play video games, read books, do some html coding and contribute in a fan-made wiki for a video game. I'm also a content creator on some (really low) level. And whenever I'm at a train station I take the opportunity and photograph trains, but I don't really post them.

Interests

Vehicles: tanks, cars, trains, even a little about planes. I'm also interested in computers and coding.

My task in Bricktopia

I started as a moderator, now my task is managing channels, so what channels exist, what channels get removed etc. I don't work alone on this, I work together with our German Community Representative, our Marketplace Operator etc. I also make the "Staff list" on Discord and now I became the developer of this website.

Motto

"If you need to ruin it, then ruin it as soon as you can." So don't hesitate, just don't question it and do it before you overthink it. If it turns out good then it is good that you did not hesitate, if it turns out bad then you faced it sooner so it ends sooner. I can't work this way 100% of the time, but in most cases I can.
