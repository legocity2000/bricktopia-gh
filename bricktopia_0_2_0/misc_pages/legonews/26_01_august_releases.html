2026 August releases

Release dates are valid for EU. Release dates outside EU may vary from the shown dates.
Prices are only shown in USD, in the future we'll expand it to EUR.

11381 Jaguer E-Type

Icons; 1673 pieces

Lego releases a new set for the Icons car series on August 1st, which is the E type. Lego made the car in its iconic British Racing Green colour and its cabriolet version. The car features opening doors, a detailed engine, working steering and a toolkit in the trunk, making it perfect for classic car enthusiasts.

139.99 USD

lego.com article

11382 Hubble Space Telescope

Icons; 1252 pieces

Following the 10360 Shuttle Carrier, Lego has turned another iconic space exploration tool into a set. The Hubble telescope comes with its solar panels, an openable cover for the telescope itself and a display stand, that features 3 objects, which were discovered with the Hubble.

139.99 USD

lego.com

21370 E.T.

Ideas; 1226 pieces

After releasing many Sci-Fi movie icons, such as the 10300 Time Machine or Wall-E, now it is time for E.T. The set comes with a (red) light brick and E.T.'s arms, fingers and head are movable.

139.99 USD

lego.com

Halloween sets

40872 Halloween Pumpkin Lantern, piece count: 323. This seasonal set features a Light Brick and a Ghost minifig, that glows in the dark. Its lego.com price is 29.99 USD.

40883 Halloween Skull Candle, piece count: 204. This Halloween-themed set is a nice decor for Halloween. It comes with 2 pairs of eyes: transparent-green ones and a pair of glow-in-the-dark ones. Its lego.com price is 19.99 USD.

21372 La Catrina, an Ideas set, that consists of 1212 pieces. Dive into the Mexican culture and use this set as a Day of the Dead decoration. Its lego.com price is 139.99 USD.

Botanicals

11512 Hanging Golden Pothos, piece count: 372. The plant has single- and dual-coloured leaves and comes with a pot and a pot holder lego.com price is 59.99 USD.

11513 Dark Flower Arrangement. The new flower set focuses on darker flowers, instead of ones in brighter colours like the older ones (the red or pink rose bouquet for example). Its lego.com price is 59.99.

31221 Gustav Klimt - The Kiss

Art; 4000 pieces

The largest Art set that is available right now (from August 4!) and the 5th largest overall. The artwork's copy measures at 60x54cm (23.5x21 inches) and comes with a so-called "hanging mechanism", so a hanger.

299.99 USD

lego.com

Star Wars

75454 Imperial Remnant AT-AT, 1453 pieces. The new AT-AT comes with figures from the new Mandalorian movie. Its lego.com price is 159.99 USD.

75453 Offworld Sandcrawler and Mudhorn, 1683 pieces. Another set for the Mandalorian, this time for the series. The main build is the Crawler of the Jawas. Its lego.com price is 199.99 USD.

75455 Boba Fett, 1544 pieces. An upscaled, brick-built version of Boba Fett, comes with a rifle, a stand and a minifigure as well. Its lego.com price is 169.99 USD.

Speed Champions

77263 BMW M3 E30, 358 pieces. The "old" M3 in the classic "M" livery. Its lego.com price is 29.99 USD.

77264 Jaguar Project 7 & Land Rover Defender, 740 pieces. A Speed champions duo set, now for the first time featuring 2 cars from different manufacturers. And the Defender is a surprise, since it is not a race or sport car, but an offroader. Its lego.com price is 59.99 USD.

Technic

42238 Ducati Desmo450, 457 pieces. The new technic moto is Ducati's offroad motorcycle with a stand. Its lego.com price is 49.99 USD.

42239 Batmobile Tumbler, 719 pieces. The new Control plus set features an unkown amount of engines (but I think 2 or 3) and goes for 189.99 USD on lego.com.

Technic cars

42234 Dodge Viper GTS-R, 805 pieces. The Viper has working steering, doors and its V10 engine with moving pistons. The set also contains a bonus code for the video game Forza Horizon 6. The set's price is 64.99 USD at lego.com.

42235 Ferrari 488 Pista, 882 pieces. While it has the same 54.99 USD price on lego.com as the Viper and the 2 cars share their mechanical functions, this one has more pieces.

42236 Custom Garage Ford Mustang GT, 973 pieces. Lego's new Mustang set slightly reminds me of the Creator Expert Mustang with its concept: modifications: factory spec or racing kit? The Builder's choice. The set goes for 99.99 USD at lego.com.

Pokémon

72150 Munchlax, 757 pieces. The small diorama features this Pokémon's favourite thing: food and helps with displaying this 69.99 USD (lego.com) set.

72160 Arcanine, 1190 pieces. The figure has movable body parts, so you can try recreating its fast runner pose. On lego.com you can buy it for 109.99 USD.

72168 Rayquaza, 1083 pieces. To display this legendary Pokémon you'll need a 38cm tall space. Its lego.com price is 129.99 USD.

Misc

Creator 31394, Red Panda, 297 pieces. This 3in1 set has instructions for a Panda, an Owl and a Clownfish. Its lego.com price is 29.99 USD.

Kpop Demon Hunters 72537, Derpy Tiger and Sussie Bird, 825 pieces. The legs of the creature are movable and the Builder can choose from 2 expressions to build the face with. Its lego.com price is 69.99 USD.

40873 Colorful Lantern, 286 pieces. This set is perfect as a decoration into a fitting environment. Its lego.com price is 19.99 USD.

One Piece

75646 Garp's Marine Battleship, 1705 pieces. The ship has a bunch of cannons and the 4 cloth sails. Its lego.com price is 179.99 USD.

75645 Battle at Drum Castle, 1038 pieces. The castle is openable and has a detailed inside. Its lego.com price is 109.99 USD.

75644 Dorry vs. Brogy – Giants of Little Garden, 733 pieces. The 2 big figures are great for displays or playing. Its lego.com price is 79.99 USD.

One Piece

75641 Dr. Hiriluk's Hideout, 271 pieces. The hut's interrior and exterrior are well detailed. Its lego.com price is 29.99 USD.

75642 Showdown with Captain Smoker, 547 pieces. The blue building's windows can be kicked out, this adds new play features. Its lego.com price is 69.99 USD.

75643 Tony Tony Chopper, 577 pieces. The figure comes with a stand and accessories. Its lego.com price is 69.99 USD.

75647 Gum-Gum Fruit, 482 pieces. The display set comes with a minifigure and a sign on the side of the display stand. Its lego.com price is 69.99 USD.
